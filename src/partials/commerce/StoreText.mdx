By default, a product is sent to {props.platform} with its Odoo name and description. When one store needs different text (a longer title for search, a description written for that shop), set it on the store product only.

1. Open **Online Stores > Listings** (or the **Listings** tab of the product) and click the store product.
2. Open the **Store Text** tab.
3. Fill **Title in This Store** and/or **Description in This Store**.
4. Click **Update in Store**.

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Title in This Store** | Sent instead of the Odoo product name, to this store only. Empty: the product name. |
| **Description in This Store** | Sent instead of the Odoo description, to this store only. Empty: the product description. |

The Odoo product is not changed, and other stores keep receiving the Odoo text. Changes to the title are kept in the store product's history (chatter).
