What Odoo does with an imported order depends on how it was paid. Each payment method {props.platform} reports (for example a card gateway, bank transfer or cash on delivery) gets a line in **Online Stores > Configuration > Payment Methods**, created automatically the first time an order uses it.

### Fields

| Field | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Store** | | The store the method belongs to. Each store has its own methods. |
| **Name** | The name {props.platform} sends | Shown in the order's **Online Store** tab. |
| **Store Code** | The code {props.platform} sends | How Odoo recognises the method. Do not change it. |
| **Confirm Order** | On | Confirm the quotation into a sales order. |
| **Validate Delivery** | Off | Validate the delivery right away when every product is in stock. For digital goods, services or stock shipped by a third party. |
| **Create and Post Invoice** | On | Create the invoice and post it. |
| **Register Payment When Paid** | On when the store has a **Default Payment Journal** | Register the payment on the invoice in the journal below. |
| **Payment Journal** | The store's **Default Payment Journal** | Bank or cash journal of the payment. Required when **Register Payment When Paid** is on. |
| **Payment Term** | Empty | Payment term of orders paid this way. Wins over the store's **Payment Term**. |

### What happens, in this order

Each time an order is imported or updated, Odoo runs these steps. A step that is already done is skipped.

1. **Confirm**, when **Confirm Order** is on and the store status is one the connector treats as confirmed (paid, processing, on hold or completed). Unpaid orders the connector imports as *pending* stay quotations.
2. **Validate the delivery**, when **Validate Delivery** is on and every product can be reserved. If something is missing, the delivery stays open and Odoo tries again at the next update of the order. A delivery that needs a decision (for example lots or serial numbers) is left for you, with a warning in the log.
3. **Invoice**, when **Create and Post Invoice** is on and the order is **paid in the store** or **completed**. An order is invoiced once: credit notes of later refunds never lead to a second invoice.
4. **Register the payment**, when **Register Payment When Paid** is on, a journal is set and the order is paid in the store.

Without a payment method (some orders have none, for example a free order), Odoo confirms the order and invoices it once it is paid or completed, but registers no payment.

:::note
A step that fails (for example an invoice that cannot be posted because of a missing account) does not stop the import. The order is kept, the failed step is listed in the log as a warning, and it is tried again with the next update of the order. Click **Update From Store** on the order to try right away.
:::

### Recommended setups

| Payment method | Confirm | Validate Delivery | Invoice | Register Payment | Journal |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Card gateway (paid at checkout) | On | Off | On | On | A bank or clearing journal for that gateway |
| Bank transfer | On | Off | On | On | Your bank journal (the payment is registered when the store marks the order paid) |
| Cash on delivery | On | Off | On | On | A cash or courier journal. The invoice is created once the store marks the order paid or completed. |
| Marketplace or gift card paid elsewhere | On | Off | On | Off | Reconcile the payout yourself |

### FAQ

**Why is my order not invoiced?**
The order is not paid or completed in {props.platform} yet, or **Create and Post Invoice** is off for its payment method, or the order is already invoiced. Check the order's **Online Store** tab (**Paid in the Store**, **Store Status**) and the log.

**Can I change the settings later?**
Yes. New settings apply to the next import or update of each order. Orders already invoiced are not touched.

**A method appears twice.**
{props.platform} sends a different code (for example after you renamed or reinstalled a payment app). Set both lines the same way.
