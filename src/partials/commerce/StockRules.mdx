Odoo is the master of stock: it sends {props.platform} the quantity of every linked product. Stock is sent only for products in **Product Links** with **Send Stock** on.

### The quantity sent

1. Odoo takes the quantity of the **Stock Source** in the **Stock From Warehouses** (or the **Order Warehouse**): *free to use* (on hand minus reserved), *on hand* or *forecasted*.
2. It applies the **Stock Buffer**.
3. It rounds down to whole units. Negative quantities become 0.

| Stock Source quantity | Stock Buffer | Buffer Value | Sent |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12 | Send the full quantity | | 12 |
| 12 | Keep back a fixed quantity | 2 | 10 |
| 1 | Keep back a fixed quantity | 2 | 0 |
| 12 | Send a percentage | 80 | 9 (9.6 rounded down) |
| 2.5 kg | Send the full quantity | | 2 |

:::note
On Odoo 18, 19 and 20, **Stock From Warehouses** is respected from Commerce Bridge 1.0.2. Version 1.0.1 sent the stock of all warehouses of the company there.
:::

### When stock is sent

| When | What is sent |
|---|---|
| Stock moves in Odoo (a delivery or receipt is validated, products are reserved or unreserved, an inventory adjustment is applied) | About one minute later, one job sends every linked product whose quantity changed since it was last sent. Many moves within that minute become one job. |
| Every hour (the store's **Schedules** tab) | The same check, as a safety net. |
| **Send All Stock** on the store, or **Sync... > Send stock to the store** | Every linked product, also when nothing changed. |
| **Product Links**, select lines, **Action > Send Stock to Store** | The selected products, also when nothing changed. |

Automatic sending needs **Send Stock Automatically** on and the store **Connected**. A test copy of the database never sends stock.

### Reading Product Links

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Send Stock** | Switch off to leave the quantity of this product to {props.platform}, for example for a product you manage only in the store. |
| **Stock Sent** | The quantity {props.platform} accepted last time |
| **Stock Sent On** | When |

The filters **Stock Not Sent** and **Out of Stock in Store** help to check what customers see.

### Setting Odoo stock from the store

When you start, your stock may only be correct in {props.platform}. **Sync... > Set Odoo stock from the store quantities** sets the on-hand quantity of every linked product, in the location you choose, to the store quantity, as an inventory adjustment. Use it once, at the start: afterwards Odoo is the master and sends its quantities.
