Besides product lines, an imported order can have shipping, discount and fee lines. Each uses a product, so they land on the right accounts and reports.

### Shipping

- Every shipping method {props.platform} uses gets a line in **Online Stores > Configuration > Shipping Methods** (name and **Store Code**) the first time it appears.
- Set an **Odoo Product** on a method to book its shipping lines on that product, for example one product per carrier. Empty: the store's **Shipping Product** (*Shipping (online store)*, `CB-SHIP`).
- Set an **Odoo Delivery Method** on a method to put that carrier on the order and its delivery (carrier name and tracking on the delivery slip, carrier reports). The shipping price stays the one the store charged.
- The line amount is the shipping price after shipping discounts, without tax, with the tax the store charged on shipping.
- Without any shipping product, the order waits with *Set a shipping product on the store.*

### Discounts

Product lines are imported at their price **before** discounts. The discounts (discount codes, automatic discounts, coupons) become discount lines with the **Discount Product** (*Discount (online store)*, `CB-DISC`), with a negative price:

- one discount line per combination of taxes, so each discount reduces the right tax (a store with 13% and 5% products gets two discount lines);
- labelled *Discount*, or *Discount (CODE1, CODE2)* with the codes used; the codes are also shown in **Coupons** on the order's **Online Store** tab.

:::tip
Separate discount lines keep the product prices clean in your sales reports, and show the total discount given per order.
:::

### Fees

Fee lines (sent by some platforms, for example a payment surcharge or gift wrap) use the **Fee Product** (*Fee (online store)*, `CB-FEE`). Without a fee product, such an order waits with *Set a fee product on the store.*

### Tips and duties

- A tip the customer added at checkout becomes a line with the **Tip Product** (*Tip (online store)*, `CB-TIP`), at the tip amount, without tax.
- Import duties charged on a cross-border order become a line with the **Duties Product** (*Duties (online store)*, `CB-DUTIES`).
- Both are services, so they are invoiced with the order but never delivered. Without them the order total would differ from the store.

### Custom items, deleted products and gift cards

- A line without a product in {props.platform} (a Shopify custom item added by hand, or a product deleted in the store after the order) uses the store's **Custom Item Product** (*Custom item (online store)*, `CB-CUSTOM`), with the store's line name and price. Set **Custom Item Product (shipped)** to use a storable product for custom items that need shipping.
- Gift cards sold in the store use the **Gift Card Product** (*Gift card (online store)*, `CB-GIFTCARD`), a service.
- Without such a product, the order waits with *Set a custom item product on the store (Orders tab).*

### Good to know

- The products are created when the app is installed. You can rename them, change their accounts and taxes, or choose other products on the store.
- Change the products before the first import: existing orders keep their lines.
