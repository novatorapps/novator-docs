With **Send Credit Notes to the Store as Refunds** on, posting a credit note for an imported order creates a refund in {props.platform}.

1. Create the credit note as usual: open the invoice, click **Credit Note**, adjust the lines (keep only the refunded products and quantities), and post it.
2. When it is posted, Odoo queues a job *Send refund ...* (one per credit note). The credit note is found from its lines' sales order, or from the invoice it reverses.
3. When the job is done, the refund appears on the order's **Online Store** tab with the direction **Sent to the store**.

A credit note is sent once. Credit notes that came from {props.platform} refunds are never sent back. In a test copy of the database, nothing is sent.
