### What is included

- **A free setup call** with every purchase: we connect your first store with you and check the settings. Write to **support@novator.co** to book it.
- **Help by email** at **support@novator.co** with questions about the app and how to use it.
- **Bug fixes** for the supported Odoo versions of the app ({props.versions}). Fixes are released as new versions on the Odoo Apps Store; download and upgrade as described in the installation guide.

### What is not included

- Custom development or changes for your own workflow (we can quote them separately).
- Problems caused by other apps, themes or plugins, in Odoo or in {props.platform}.
- Problems of the store itself, the hosting, or the {props.platform} account and plan.
- Odoo versions the app does not support, and Odoo Online (it does not allow custom apps).

### When you write

Include, so we can help on the first reply:

1. Your Odoo version and edition, and where Odoo runs (Odoo.sh or your own server).
2. The app version (**Apps**, open the app).
3. The {props.platform} version or plan.
4. What you did, what you expected and what happened, with screenshots.
5. For a sync problem: the **Error** of the job (**Queue > Jobs**, open the job) and the store order or product number.

Never send passwords, API keys or tokens by email.
