Odoo can send prices to {props.platform} for every linked product with **Send Price** on (in **Product Links**).

### The price sent

- **Regular price**: the store **Pricelist** price for one unit, or the product's **Sales Price** when the store has no pricelist.
- **Sale price**: when the **Sale Price Pricelist** gives a lower price for one unit, that price is sent as the sale (reduced) price. Otherwise the sale price is cleared.
- Prices are rounded to two decimals, in the currency of the pricelist.

### When prices are sent

| When | What is sent |
|---|---|
| Every hour, with **Send Prices Automatically** on | Products whose regular or sale price changed since they were last sent |
| **Sync... > Send prices to the store** | Every linked product |
| **Product Links**, select lines, **Action > Send Price to Store** | The selected products |

**Price Sent** and **Sale Price Sent** in **Product Links** show what {props.platform} accepted last time. A test copy of the database never sends prices.

:::tip
To run a sale, put the reduced prices in the **Sale Price Pricelist** (for example with a date range on the rules). The hourly check sends them when they start and clears them when they end.
:::
