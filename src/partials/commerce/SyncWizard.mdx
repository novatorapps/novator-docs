**Sync...** on a store (or **Online Stores > Queue > Sync With Store**) starts a bulk operation in the background. Choose the store and the operation, then click **Run in Background**. Follow the progress in **Queue > Jobs**.

<img src={props.img + '/sync_wizard.png'} alt="The Sync With Store window" />

| Operation | What it does | Options |
|---|---|---|
| **Import products from the store** | Reads the catalog page by page and imports each product: linked, matched by SKU, or created. | **Update Already Linked Products**: also read products that are already linked (off: only new ones). |
| **Import orders from the store** | Imports orders created or changed since the date. Orders already in Odoo are updated. | **Orders Changed Since** (default: today 00:00). Use it to catch up after a pause, or to import older orders once. |
| **Import customers from the store** | Imports all store customers that are not linked yet. | |
| **Set Odoo stock from the store quantities** | Sets the on-hand quantity of linked products in the chosen location to the store quantity (inventory adjustment). Use it once, when you start. | **Stock Location** (default: the stock location of the **Order Warehouse**) |
| **Send stock to the store** | Sends the quantity of every linked product, also when it did not change. | |
| **Send prices to the store** | Sends the price of every linked product, also when it did not change. | |

The store must be **Connected** (test the connection first). In a test copy of the database the two *Send* operations are refused.
