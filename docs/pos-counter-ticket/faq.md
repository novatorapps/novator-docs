# FAQ and troubleshooting

### Do I need special hardware?

No. Tickets print on any Odoo-supported receipt printer, or through the browser on a normal printer. Any USB or Bluetooth barcode scanner that types like a keyboard works at the checkout.

### Scanning a ticket does nothing

Check that scanning is not switched off on that Point of Sale, and that the scanner sends an Enter after the code (most do by default).

### "This ticket is already paid" or "in another till"

The ticket was paid by another order, or another till has it in an open order. A manager can check it under **Point of Sale > Counter Tickets**.

### Upgrading from POS Bakery Ticket?

It is the same app with a new name. Upgrade the module: your tickets, settings and printed barcodes keep working.

## Still stuck?

Write to **support@novator.co** with your Odoo version, the app version (Apps, open the app) and a screenshot.
