# Pro features

AI Connector Pro ($149, one-time) installs on top of the free app; your connections stay. [Get it on the Apps Store](https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/20.0/novator_mcp_pro).

## Approvals

**AI Connector > Approval Rules** decides which AI changes wait for a person: for example confirming orders, posting invoices, changing prices or deleting records. **Add Suggested Rules** creates sensible ones for the apps you have.

![Approval rules](/img/ai-connector/approval_rules.png)

When the AI wants to do something that needs approval, it is queued under **Approvals** and the approver gets an activity. Open it and click **Approve and Do It** or **Reject**. The change runs as the person who asked, with their access rights.

![AI changes waiting for approval](/img/ai-connector/approvals.png)

![An AI change waiting for approval](/img/ai-connector/approval_form.png)

## Change history and undo

**Change History** records every change the AI made, with the old and new values. Click **Undo** to put a record back as it was.

![Change history with undo](/img/ai-connector/change_history.png)

## Access rules per team

**Access Rules** limit the AI further for chosen users or teams: **hidden document types**, **hidden fields**, **tools not allowed** and **daily limits per user**.

![Access rule for the sales team](/img/ai-connector/access_rule.png)

## Workflows

**Workflows** are ready-made instructions the AI app offers as shortcuts: *Sales report*, *Quotations to follow up*, *Overdue invoices follow-up*, *Pipeline clean-up*, *What to reorder*, *Late vendor deliveries*, *Month-end check*, *Contact clean-up* and *Document to Odoo*. Edit them or add your own.

![Ready-made workflows](/img/ai-connector/workflows.png)
