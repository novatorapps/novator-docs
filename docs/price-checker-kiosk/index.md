# Price Checker Kiosk

Mount a tablet or touchscreen with a barcode scanner in your store. Customers scan any product and see its **price, photo, description and stock**, live from Odoo, in your colours and with your logo.

<span className="version-pill">Odoo 16.0 - 20.0</span> <span className="version-pill">Community &amp; Enterprise</span> <span className="version-pill">$249, one-time</span>

![A product on the kiosk](/img/price-checker-kiosk/kiosk_product.png)

- **Fewer questions at the counter**: customers check prices themselves.
- **Always the current price**: prices, promotions and stock come live from Odoo.
- **Any screen you already have**: it runs in a web browser, no app to install.

The tablet needs a network connection to your Odoo server.
