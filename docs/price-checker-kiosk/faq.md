# FAQ and troubleshooting

### Which tablets or screens work?

Any device with a modern browser: Android or Windows tablets, iPads, touchscreen PCs.

### A product is not found

Check that it has the scanned barcode or internal reference, is available for sale, and belongs to the kiosk's company.

### The kiosk shows a message about a new link after an upgrade

Open the kiosk in Odoo, copy its private link and open it once on the tablet.

### Does it work offline?

No. The kiosk reads prices live from your Odoo server.

## Still stuck?

Write to **support@novator.co** with your Odoo version, the app version (Apps, open the app) and a screenshot.
