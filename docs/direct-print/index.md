# Direct Print

Send invoices, delivery slips, picking lists and ZPL labels from Odoo **straight to your office and label printers**. A small agent on a computer in your network prints them. No monthly fee, no cloud print service, and your documents never leave your network.

<span className="version-pill">Odoo 13.0 - 20.0</span> <span className="version-pill">Community &amp; Enterprise</span> <span className="version-pill">$99, one-time</span>

![A delivery slip sent straight to the printer](/img/direct-print/print_sent.png)

- **Fewer clicks**: no download, open and print. The document comes out of the printer.
- **Every report**, including your own, as PDF or ZPL.
- **The right printer for every document**: per user, per workstation, by rules, or automatically when a document changes status.
