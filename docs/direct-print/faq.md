# FAQ and troubleshooting

### Is there a monthly fee?

No. You pay for the app once. There is no print service subscription and no account to create.

### Do documents leave our network?

No. Odoo creates the PDF or ZPL; the agent on your computer downloads it from your Odoo and prints it. Nothing passes through Novator or any other service.

### The computer with the agent was off

Jobs wait in Odoo and print when the agent is back. A job sent to the agent but not confirmed within 10 minutes is marked failed, so nothing is printed twice by surprise. Send it again from **Print Jobs**.

### A printer shows "Not found"

The agent no longer sees it: check that the printer is on and still installed on that computer, then restart the agent.

### Can I still download a report?

Yes. Switch Direct Print off in the printer menu, or leave your printer empty for reports without a rule.

### Does it work on Odoo Online?

No. Odoo Online does not allow third-party apps. Use Odoo.sh or your own server.

## Still stuck?

Write to **support@novator.co** with your Odoo version, the agent version and a screenshot of the print job.
